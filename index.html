Reliable collection and transport for recovered fats and oils

Ngobeni Chain Supply connects businesses that produce used cooking oil or animal fats with industrial customers who can use these materials as raw materials.

Request a Collection Request a Quote

For Suppliers

Restaurants, hotels, caterers, butcheries and food businesses can contact NCS when they have fats or used cooking oil available for collection.

For Industrial Customers

Manufacturers can enquire about fats, oils and transport services that may support soap, biodiesel, cosmetics or chemical production.

Professional Service

The business focuses on safe transport, clear communication, reliable scheduling and long-term B2B relationships.

Why this website is needed

NCS is still a proposed business, so it needs one clear online place where suppliers and customers can understand the business. The website helps the company build trust, explain its process and collect useful enquiries.

Main website goal

To create a professional and user-friendly online presence that introduces the business, explains collection and transport services, and makes communication easier for suppliers and industrial customers.
