Supplier Collection Request

This page is for businesses that have used cooking oil or fats available for collection.

Who can use this service?

Potential suppliers include restaurants, hotels, catering businesses, food-processing companies and butcheries. The goal is to make it easier for these businesses to contact NCS instead of relying only on informal communication.

Container and safety guidelines

To make collection safe and quick, please prepare your material like this:

  • Let used cooking oil cool completely before pouring it into a container.
  • Use sealed, leak-proof containers and keep different materials separate.
  • Keep the containers in a clean, easy-to-reach place for the collection team.
  • Do not mix oil with water, chemicals or food waste.
  • Label each container with the material type and approximate quantity.
